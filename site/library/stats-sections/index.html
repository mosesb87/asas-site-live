How to choose stats & counters

“500+” means nothing on its own. The stats sections that work pair every figure with a short label and sit it next to the claim it supports, so the reader never has to guess what was measured.

A row of three or four counters — after the hero or the features, when you have a few results of equal weight: clients, projects, years, rating. Keep the labels to two or three words.

One big number beside a claim — when a single figure carries the argument — a saving, a speed, a percentage. The number is the headline; the paragraph beside it explains it.

Stats over an image — for firms whose work is physical — construction, events, manufacturing. The photo shows the work; the figures say how much of it there is.

What is inside the block

An Arqam block is a container with inner containers for each figure, built on Elementor’s free counter widget (which animates from zero when scrolled into view) plus a heading or text for the label. Change the ending number, the suffix (+, %, k) and the label; the animation takes care of itself.

Import into Elementor

  1. Download the block (or your whole stacked page) from the Studio as a .json file.
  2. In WordPress go to Templates → Saved Templates and click Import Templates.
  3. Choose the file. It appears under Saved Templates with its ASAS name.
  4. Open any page in Elementor, click the folder icon, choose My Templates, and insert it.

Questions

Stats & counters, answered.

Do the counters animate?

Yes. They use Elementor’s free counter widget, which counts up from a starting number when the section scrolls into view. You set the start, the end, the duration and any prefix or suffix.

Can I use a figure that is not a number, like “24/7”?

Use a heading widget instead of the counter for that figure. It will not animate, but it keeps the same size and position as the counters beside it.

Are these templates free?

Yes. Browsing, stacking and colouring need no account. Downloading the Elementor JSON needs a free account — a name, an email and a password, no card.

Do I need Elementor Pro?

Most blocks use core Elementor widgets only. Where a block uses a Pro widget, the Studio names it before you download, because Elementor silently drops widgets it doesn’t have on import.

How do I import a block into Elementor?

Download the .json file, open Templates → Saved Templates in WordPress, click Import Templates, choose the file, then insert it on any page from the editor’s template library.
