How to choose faq sections

Visitors open an FAQ when they are close to deciding. The layout should get them to their answer in one move, not hide it behind decoration.

An accordion — when you have eight or more questions. One answer open at a time keeps the section short, and Elementor’s accordion writes the heading structure search engines expect.

A two-column list — when you have four to six questions and every answer is short. Showing everything at once beats making people click.

A split layout — when one side should carry a contact prompt — “still unsure? talk to us” — next to the answers. Useful directly above a form.

What is inside the block

An Asila block is a container with a heading group and either Elementor’s accordion or toggle widget, or a grid of heading-and-text pairs. Questions are real headings, so they read correctly to screen readers and can be marked up as FAQPage schema by your SEO plugin.

Import into Elementor

  1. Download the block (or your whole stacked page) from the Studio as a .json file.
  2. In WordPress go to Templates → Saved Templates and click Import Templates.
  3. Choose the file. It appears under Saved Templates with its ASAS name.
  4. Open any page in Elementor, click the folder icon, choose My Templates, and insert it.

Questions

FAQ sections, answered.

Do the FAQ sections work with FAQ schema?

The questions are real headings and answers are plain text, which is what SEO plugins like Rank Math and Yoast need to generate FAQPage markup. ASAS doesn’t inject schema itself.

Accordion or open list — which is better?

Open lists for up to six short answers; accordions beyond that. The Studio lets you swap one for the other in place and keep the rest of the page.

Are these templates free?

Yes. Browsing, stacking and colouring need no account. Downloading the Elementor JSON needs a free account — a name, an email and a password, no card.

Do I need Elementor Pro?

Most blocks use core Elementor widgets only. Where a block uses a Pro widget, the Studio names it before you download, because Elementor silently drops widgets it doesn’t have on import.

How do I import a block into Elementor?

Download the .json file, open Templates → Saved Templates in WordPress, click Import Templates, choose the file, then insert it on any page from the editor’s template library.
